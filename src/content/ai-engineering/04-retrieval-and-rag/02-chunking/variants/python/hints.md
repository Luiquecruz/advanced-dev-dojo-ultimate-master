Work with ranges (`(start, end)` tuples into the original text) instead of strings, and slice only when you build the chunk dicts. Offsets then stay exact for free. `chunk_fixed` is a `for start in range(0, len(text), size - overlap)` loop with a `break` once a window's end reaches `len(text)`.
---
For `chunk_recursive`, write a recursive `split_range(text, start, end, size, separators)`: return `[(start, end)]` when it fits; otherwise find each occurrence of `separators[0]` with `text.find(separator, position)` between `start` and `end`, cut right after it, and call `split_range` again on every piece with `separators[1:]`. With no separators left, cut windows of `size`.
---
Merging keeps a list `current` of pieces. Before adding a piece, check `piece[1] - current[0][0] > size`; if so, append `(current[0][0], last_end)` as a chunk, then rebuild `current` from its own tail: walk `reversed(current)` while `last_end - candidate[0] <= overlap` and `piece[1] - candidate[0] <= size`. Trim each merged range with two `while` loops over `text[index].isspace()`.
---
For `chunk_markdown`, collect headings with `re.finditer(r"^(#{1,6})[ \t]+(.+?)[ \t]*$", text, re.MULTILINE)`: `match.start()` is the section start, `match.end()` where its body begins, `len(match.group(1))` the level and `match.group(2)` the title. Keep a stack of `{"level", "title"}` entries, pop while the top's level is `>=` the new level, push, and run the recursive range logic between this heading and the next one. Skip a section when the text after its heading line is blank (`.strip() == ""`).
