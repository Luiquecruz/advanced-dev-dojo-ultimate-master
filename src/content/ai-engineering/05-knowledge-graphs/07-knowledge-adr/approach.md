1. Turn the competency questions into the ontology: each question is a path of relations between classes, and the longest path sets the traversal depth. Anything no question needs can wait.
2. Classify the questions: entity questions are answered by traversal, the portfolio-wide question needs pre-computed community summaries, and the metric question belongs to the semantic layer, not to the graph or the model.
3. Map each compliance and data-quality sentence of the briefing to a safeguard: validation against the ontology, entity resolution, provenance and a rejected log.
4. Choose the representation from the constraints: no external exchange and an existing property graph database favor a property graph; multi-hop and global questions rule out vector-only retrieval.
5. Size the indexing: freshness sets a minimum number of rebuilds, the budget sets a maximum. Compute the projection with the briefing's formula and keep it in the plan.
6. Write the record with the forces and numbers in the context, one "We will" sentence, alternatives tied to the facts that rejected them, and honest negative consequences (ontology ownership, summary staleness, resolution mistakes).
