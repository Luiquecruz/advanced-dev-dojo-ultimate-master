Keep issued credentials in a `Map` keyed by token inside the broker's closure; `randomUUID()` from `node:crypto` makes a good token. `verify` is four early returns in the order of the contract. Remember that a credential is expired when `now() >= expiresAt`, not `>`.
---
`resolvePlaceholders` is a small recursive function: strings get `value.replace(/\{\{secret:([A-Za-z0-9_]+)\}\}/g, (_, name) => ...)`, arrays get `map`, plain objects get `Object.fromEntries(Object.entries(value).map(...))`, everything else is returned. For redaction, build a list of `{ name, form }` for each secret and its three forms (`Buffer.from(value).toString('base64')`, `encodeURIComponent(value)`), sort by `form.length` descending, and replace with `split(form).join(...)`.
