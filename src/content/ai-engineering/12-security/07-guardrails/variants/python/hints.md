Do the two checksums first; they are pure loops. For Luhn, `enumerate(reversed(digits))` gives each digit with its position from the right; double when `index % 2 == 1`. For CPF, one helper computes a check digit from a list of numbers with weights starting at `len(digits) + 1`; call it on the first 9 digits and on the first 10. `re.fullmatch(r"[0-9]+", digits)` rejects anything that is not digits.
---
Starting patterns (compile with `re.ASCII` so `\d` and `\w` mean ASCII, like JavaScript): phone `(?<![\w+])(?:\+\d{1,3}[\s.-]?(?:\(\d{2,3}\)|\d{2,3})|\(\d{2,3}\))[\s.-]?\d{3,5}[\s.-]?\d{4}(?!\d)`, card `(?<!\d)\d(?:[ -]?\d){12,18}(?!\d)`, CPF `(?<!\d)\d{3}\.?\d{3}\.?\d{3}-?\d{2}(?!\d)`. For cards and CPFs, the `sub` callback strips non-digits with `re.sub(r"\D", "", value)`, validates, and returns the original match when invalid.
---
`classify_input` is a `for` loop over attempts with a `try`/`except (ModelError, ValueError)` that just continues; inside, `continue` when `"finishReason"` is not `"stop"`, then `json.loads(response["text"])` and return the `"label"` only if it is `"safe"` or `"unsafe"`. `guarded_answer` is a sequence of early returns in the order of the table; build the topic check with `re.search(rf"\b{re.escape(keyword)}\b", text, re.IGNORECASE)`.
